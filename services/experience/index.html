Insights

Practical guidance for leaders planning their first AI projects.

How to choose your first AI use case5 min read

The best first project is small, frequent and measurable. It should prove value quickly and teach your team how to work with AI.

  • Pick a process that repeats at least weekly
  • Choose one with a clear cost or time baseline
  • Avoid processes with no accessible data or named owner
  • Define the KPI and a stop rule before building
What a WhatsApp assistant should and should not handle4 min read

A good assistant is clear about its limits. Let it answer routine questions fast, and hand over everything that needs judgement.

  • Handle: opening hours, prices, order status, bookings and lead capture
  • Hand over: complaints, refunds, legal questions and any request to speak to a person
  • Answer only from approved content
  • Log every conversation and review samples weekly
What an AI pilot must prove before you scale5 min read

A pilot is an experiment with a decision at the end. Agree the pass mark before it starts.

  • The KPI beat the baseline on real data
  • The error rate and exception volume are known
  • Users accept the tool and use it
  • Security and data owners have signed off
  • The running cost per transaction is understood
A data readiness checklist for AI projects4 min read

Most delays come from data, not models. Ask these questions in the first week.

  • Where does the data live and who owns it?
  • Is it digital, structured enough and up to date?
  • Can we reach it through an export or an API?
  • Does it contain personal data that needs extra controls?
  • Who corrects errors when we find them?
